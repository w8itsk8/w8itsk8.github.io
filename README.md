# w8itsk8.github.io


I started my computer science degree at SNHU after realizing that I didn't have a particular passion for anything other than making the world better. At some point it occurred to me that the skills gained in this degree program in particular are ones that are more than useful in any industry, and the data analysis concentration would allow me to learn how to make complex informative graphics, which is something I've always had an interest in. 

To my surprise, this agree was only about half technical skills. The other half consisted of project planning, professional communication of complex ideas to audiences unfamiliar with the technical side of the project in words and graphics, and learning how to use existing patterns to solve complex coding problems others have faced. These skills have changed my approach to other projects and problems in my life, from communications with employers and clients to adapting art projects for exactly what I want. While I don't imagine myself in the technology field, working with large volumes of data to draw meaningful conclusions and the ability to create mobile applications and websites from the ground up are skills that will place me far ahead in terms of applicable hard skills. Combined with my professional experience managing people and ensuring data quality, I feel confident in my ability to perform the tasks in the kind of position I'm looking for.



At the moment, my portfolio only contains a single project. I elected to spend my time and energy this term to make one major project into a project of a quality you might actually see on the market. Though the single project doesn't show my full array of potential languages and skills, what it does show is my ability to see a need in the world and see it fulfilled end-to-end. They'll see the languages and technologies that I'm familiar with on my resume, but creativity, passion, attention to details, and endurance through difficult problems are traits that are hard to list, but that can, hopefully, be communicated though this project's functionality.
